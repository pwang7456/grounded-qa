员工手册 · 假期篇 Employee Handbook - Leave Policy

第一章 年假 Chapter 1 Annual Leave

年假天数按司龄计算：入职满 1 年不满 10 年的员工，每年享有 15 天带薪年假；司龄满 10 年不满 20 年为 20 天；满 20 年为 25 天。Annual leave is granted by tenure: employees with 1 to 10 years of service receive 15 paid annual leave days per year; 10 to 20 years receive 20 days; 20 years or more receive 25 days.

年假须在当年 12 月 31 日前使用，未休完的年假最多可结转 5 天至次年第一季度，逾期自动作废。Annual leave must be used before December 31 of the current year; up to 5 unused days may be carried over to Q1 of the next year, after which they expire.

申请 3 天以内（含）年假由直属主管审批；3 天以上需部门总监审批，至少提前 5 个工作日在 HR 系统提交。Requests of up to 3 days are approved by the direct manager; longer requests need director approval and must be filed at least 5 working days in advance in the HR system.

新入职员工自入职之日起满 3 个月后即可开始休年假，当年年假按剩余日历天数折算（不足 1 天部分不享受）。New hires may take annual leave after completing 3 months of service; the first-year entitlement is prorated by remaining calendar days.

第二章 病假 Chapter 2 Sick Leave

病假需提供二级及以上医院或公司医务室出具的病假证明。15 天以内的病假工资按基本工资的 70% 发放；超过 15 天按 60% 发放，医疗期内不得解除劳动合同。Sick leave requires a medical certificate from a qualified hospital. Pay during sick leave is 70% of base salary for up to 15 days, 60% beyond 15 days; employment may not be terminated during the medical treatment period.

突发疾病应在当日 10:00 前电话或系统报备直属主管，事后 2 个工作日内补交证明。Report sudden illness to your manager before 10:00 on the day, and submit the certificate within 2 working days.

第三章 婚假与产假 Chapter 3 Marriage and Maternity Leave

婚假 3 天，晚婚不另加；再婚同等适用。Marriage leave is 3 calendar days and applies equally to remarriage.

产假按国家标准 98 天执行，另享公司奖励假 60 天，合计 158 天；难产增加 15 天。陪产假 15 天。Maternity leave follows the national standard of 98 days plus a 60-day company bonus leave (158 days total); +15 days for cesarean delivery. Paternity leave is 15 days.

哺乳假：婴儿一周岁内每天 1 小时带薪哺乳时间。Nursing mothers with infants under one year get 1 paid hour per day.

第四章 其他假期 Chapter 4 Other Leave

丧假：直系亲属 3 天，祖父母/外祖父母 1 天。Bereavement leave: 3 days for immediate family, 1 day for grandparents.

工伤休假按劳动能力鉴定结论执行，期间工资福利不变。Work-injury leave follows the labor capability assessment; wages and benefits remain unchanged.
